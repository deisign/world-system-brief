## Method

- No changes to historical daily issue claims or evidence.
- Every relationship has a dated registration, bilingual explanation, source URL, and `physical_delta: false`.
- Comparisons with different source reports are **not** promoted to explicit causal/physical relations.
- Three additional counterpart entities are registered only for independently documented, analytically distinct mechanisms, not to manufacture node counts.
- Missing/invalid claim-source links, unknown endpoints, and unsupported external counterpart identifiers cause audit validation to fail.
- `node publisher/entity-connectivity-audit.js` generates the authoritative coverage counts and list of remaining isolates, if any.
- Coverage is **not** proof that the world graph is complete: each new edition may introduce new entities and dependencies, and a documented comparison is not a dependency.
