# Dependency dimensions · System Ledger v1

Each recorded directed relation may carry the following **optional, editorially reviewed** fields:

| Field | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| `substitutability` | What can replace the constrained flow or service, under which conditions? |
| `substitution_horizon` | Supported time range to replace it; use qualitative unknown when unverified |
| `leverage_effect` | Which actor's bargaining or operational power changes, in which direction? |
| `rent_distribution` | Which actors plausibly capture or lose economic rents; distinguish inference from measurement |
| `adaptation_route` | Verified or proposed circumvention, diversification, stock release, or rerouting mechanism |

## Editorial rules

- A populated field must be supported by the relation's `source_ids` and `claim_ids`; if evidence is absent, omit the field. The interface displays **Not established / Не встановлено**.
- Never infer substitutability or a quantitative horizon solely from the existence of two connected nodes.
- Distinguish **proposed**, **observed**, and **implemented** adaptation.
- Do not imply that a legal waiver generates more physical capacity until separately verified.
- Changes in flow, dependency, and leverage are distinct observations; do not reuse the same `confirmed_delta` status automatically.
- Use plain-language values intelligible in both languages; future schema revisions should support structured, locale-specific translations and per-field evidence IDs.

This version adds a **view and schema convention**, not speculative backfilled values. Existing source links and dated relation history remain authoritative.

## Dated assessment implementation — 5 November 2026

The first evidence-validated structured Dependency Ledger is now stored in `knowledge/dependency-assessments.json`, version `wsb-dependency-assessments-v1`. This is **not** a replacement for the daily `state.json` and its confirmed Δ rules. It is a growing set of explicitly issue-scoped structural assessments of flows, dependency, bottleneck, substitution horizon, leverage, rent and adaptation, each linked to one or more canonical entity/claim/source records in a real issued edition.

### Provenance and quality guardrails

- `classification=knowledge_update` is intentionally required. A source's newly published analysis does not authorize a physical system transition.
- The `entity_slug` must exist in `knowledge/entities.json`, the assessment date must exactly equal the cited canonical issue date, and the full English/Ukrainian content is mandatory.
- Each source ID must resolve to dated HTTPS evidence in that same issue, explicitly support the entity and be cited by a matching claim. Baselines and internal-only evidence cannot establish a new dependency assessment.
- Substitution horizons have either `status=unknown, min_years=null, max_years=null`, or `status=assessed_horizon` with positive, ordered year bounds expressly present in at least one underlying source description. The UK committee's 10–15 years are scoped to its whole de-risking programme and **must not** be portrayed as a precise plant-construction period.
- Revenue, price and profit observations remain distinct from measured rents; the first Maersk assessment uses the explicit `company_earnings_signal` category, never a fabricated monopoly-rent amount.
- Adaptation is classified as proposed or observed route adjustment. Proposed purchasing/finance facilities are **not** implemented industrial capacity.
- Records must be appended in chronological order for each entity. Previous dated assessments are not overwritten.

### First records

- `DPA-0039-UK-CRITICAL-MINERALS`: materials, concentrated Chinese refining supply chains, 10–15 year **assessed policy horizon**, proposed procurement/financing/recycling, no measured rent. Provenance: `WSB-0039-CLM-01`, sources 01–02.
- `DPA-0039-MAERSK-FREIGHT`: goods, port congestion, quarterly rate signal and 2026 EBITDA outlook, replacement horizon unknown, route adjustment observed, company guidance is **not** a market-wide rent estimate. Provenance: `WSB-0039-CLM-02`, source 03.

Publication routes: `/en|ua/ledger/dependencies/` and `/en|ua/ledger/dependencies/<slug>/`. The routes are generated by the standard site build, linked on the homepage, and included in Chromium route checks.

### Important next step

The next structural iteration should link multiple dated observations on the **same** dependency into a formal evolution chain, distinguishing: source updates, physical bottleneck shifts, change in substitutability, changes in leverage and observable bypasses. Until sourced evidence exists, keep fields explicitly unknown or proposals rather than creating synthetic transitions.
