# New tanker destruction claims remain unverified

On November 4 Iran's Revolutionary Guards stated that four oil tankers had been destroyed by mines on a restricted route south of Hormuz and that two other vessels had been seized. Reuters reported the assertion; independent verification of ship identities, mine strikes, losses and seizures was not established by this source. No physical capacity loss is classified as confirmed.
