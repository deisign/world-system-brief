# The compute layer is a physical target

Digital infrastructure depends on physical facilities. On 9 October, Yandex reported that several modules at its Kaluga-region data centre had been disabled after a drone strike. A different data-centre facility in the Ryazan region was hit on 8 October. The second affected site marks a confirmed expansion of physical damage across the company's infrastructure footprint.

That observation has limits. The available reports do not quantify the share of Yandex computing capacity lost, demonstrate a company-wide service interruption, or establish the condition of the supercomputers reportedly hosted at the Ryazan facility. A damaged site is not equivalent to a collapsed digital network.

The systemic significance lies in the dependency revealed: storage and computation are not abstract cloud functions. They require buildings, electricity, cooling, networks and physical security. Damage to a second site makes the exposure more visible, but the scale of any downstream effects is still developing.

## AN INSTITUTIONAL CHANGE, DATED CORRECTLY

US authorities announced restrictions on PERM immigration-certification processing for named technology companies on 8 October. The measure affects access to a channel for employing and retaining foreign specialists. It is a real regulatory change, but not a transition that began on 9 October. The allegations cited by authorities should not be confused with adjudicated findings. We register the policy as a prior-day development, not a second confirmed delta for this issue.
