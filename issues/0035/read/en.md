# OPEC+ keeps targets steady as export constraints persist

On November 1 seven OPEC+ producers decided to leave their December output targets unchanged. Reuters reported that export disruptions kept several Gulf producers below targets. An unchanged target is a policy continuity decision, not proof of rising physical supply; the 2027 capacity review remains pending.
