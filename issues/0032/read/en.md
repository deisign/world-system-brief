# A confirmed vessel fire is not proof of three mine strikes

On October 29 UKMTO recorded a third-party observation of one vessel on fire north of Khasab, Oman. Iran's IRGC separately claimed that three tankers struck mines; independent reporting did not confirm vessel identities, causes, or that the accounts described the same incident. Observed fire and Iranian claims remain distinct evidence classes.
