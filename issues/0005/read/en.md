# The bottleneck moved

The original energy chokepoint is easing faster than the system behind it. LNG traffic through Hormuz has recovered materially, while refined-product supply remains tight enough to activate strategic stocks.

The shortage did not disappear. It moved — and policy followed it.

## Δ 01 — Marinus moves into construction

Project Marinus marked the start of its next construction phase, with work ramping up across Tasmania and Victoria after financial close and major approvals. Stage 1 is designed as a 750 MW two-way electricity interconnector, targeted for service by 2030.

**planned interconnector → financed / approved → construction → future 750 MW transfer capacity**

Australia did not gain 750 MW today. The project crossed another implementation threshold.

## Δ 02 — G7 activates the stock buffer

The G7 moved from discussion to decision. Leaders committed to a coordinated **100 million barrel** release through the IEA over four months, beginning immediately, with a substantial diesel release frontloaded within the first 20 days. They also pledged to avoid energy export restrictions between G7 members.

**product shortage → strategic stocks → coordinated release → market supply**

This is the downstream bottleneck becoming policy. The constraint is no longer only observed in diesel prices and inventories; emergency stocks are being mobilised against it.

## Hormuz is recovering — but not normal

S&P Global counted 19 LNG shipments through Hormuz in September; Kpler counted 21, versus 15 in June. Some Qatar-linked vessels still made dark transits with AIS switched off.

**throughput ↑ · abnormal navigation persists**

The system has learned to move more cargo through the constraint without restoring the old navigation regime. This is adaptation, not normalisation.

## Kedah remains an option

Gas Malaysia targets FID in H2 2027 for an LNG import terminal in Kedah of up to 6 mtpa. It would add an import route for northern Malaysia, but a target FID is not an FID.

**alternative LNG access ↑ as an option · committed capacity unchanged**

## CHAIN OF THE DAY

**CHOKEPOINT DISRUPTION → ADAPTATION → CRUDE / LNG RECOVERY → REFINING / PRODUCTS LAG → STRATEGIC-STOCK RELEASE**

Bottlenecks are not fixed places on a map. When a system adapts around one constraint, the next weakest link becomes visible. On 2 October that downstream constraint crossed from diagnosis into intervention.
