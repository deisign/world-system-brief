# Repair becomes capacity

Germany committed roughly **€350 million** in energy support for Ukraine ahead of winter, aimed at generators, substations and repairs. In a system repeatedly hit faster than it can be rebuilt, repair finance is not merely aid: it is capacity preservation.

## Δ 01 — External capital enters the repair loop

**infrastructure damage → repair need → external funding → resilience capacity**

The delta is the commitment of resources to keep the Ukrainian energy system operating through repeated attacks and winter demand.

Ukraine and Siemens Energy also signed memoranda on infrastructure protection, underground gas-storage modernisation and power generation, including evaluation of 12 gas-turbine units. Those projects remain DEVELOPING until procurement and deployment are fixed.

## OPEC+ chooses persistence
