# Energy security shifts from production to delivery

On November 2 ADNOC chief Sultan Al Jaber said nearly one billion barrels had been drawn from global oil inventories since the Hormuz disruption and cited shipping and logistics as more than 30% of barrel cost, compared with about 5% before the war. These are attributed industry-executive estimates, not independently verified new inventory measurements.
