# Navigation risk persists despite diplomatic signals

Reuters reported on October 27 that Brent settled at $113.88 per barrel after a $1.93 decline, as diplomatic signals eased immediate expectations of supply loss but threats to Strait of Hormuz shipping persisted. The reported Houthi strike on Ras Tanura was a claim, not independent evidence of refinery damage.

## SYSTEM LEDGER

**Bottleneck:** Exposure of Hormuz shipping to attacks.

**Substitution horizon:** Not established in the cited report.
