# Fire is not yet a state change

The loudest event was a fire near an Aramco facility in Riyadh. Houthis said they had attacked the facility with missiles and drones. Reuters independently observed smoke and fire, but Saudi authorities and Aramco had not confirmed the attack or any loss of refining or export capacity.

That distinction is the issue.

**incident observed → attribution claimed → capacity impact unconfirmed**

A dramatic image is not automatically a system delta. Until throughput, operating status or a verified infrastructure loss changes, the event remains DEVELOPING.

The G7 emergency-stock decision from 2 October moved into its implementation window. Hormuz remained an adapting constraint. Neither produced a fresh transition today.
