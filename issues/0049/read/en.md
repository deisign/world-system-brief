# THE DAMAGE PRECEDES THE REPORT. CONTRACTS PRECEDE THE CARGO.

Two different systems expose the same accounting error on 17 November: documentary visibility is not the date of physical damage, and a contract award is not a delivered cargo. The UN's new statement concerns October attacks on Ukrainian power and heating infrastructure; Reuters reports Indian buyers' future US LPG contracts. The two do not form one causal chain. [WSB-0049-SRC-01; WSB-0049-SRC-03]

## 1. Ukraine: the UN report records October's physical damage, not a November 17 strike

The UN Human Rights Monitoring Mission reported **372 civilian deaths and 1,981 injuries in October 2026**. Its full monthly report was published **16 November**; a follow-up press statement was published **17 November**. The UN identified extensive coordinated attacks on electricity and heat infrastructure during **October**, disrupting electricity and sometimes water, with rolling power cuts. It did not date those damaged assets to the day the press statement came out. Civilian casualty totals cover all documented categories of attacks and cannot be treated as deaths from energy-infrastructure strikes alone. [WSB-0049-SRC-01; WSB-0049-SRC-02]

## 2. The physical dependence: heating, power and water

Power supply is a prerequisite for electrically driven pumping and many heating-system components. The UN explicitly documents both electricity and water interruptions after infrastructure damage. WSB registers the directed dependency **October energy damage → rolling electricity interruptions**, while the relationship to civilian casualties is a separately labelled **same-month context**, not a causal fatality count attributable to individual damaged facilities. No specific new 17 November megawatt loss has been established. [WSB-0049-SRC-02]

## 3. India: a 2027 LPG procurement option is contracted today

Reuters reported that Indian Oil, BPCL and HPCL awarded a tender to Saudi Aramco, Chevron, Totsa and Vitol for approximately **2.75 million tonnes of US LPG in 2027**. The plan envisages five very large gas-carrier cargoes a month, four delivered and one free-on-board. India sourced about **90% of its 2025 LPG imports from the Middle East**, making geographic diversification material to the procurement model. These are market-participant reports of awards, not verified 17 November vessel departures, terminal receipts or extra production. LPG is distinct from LNG. [WSB-0049-SRC-03]

## SYSTEM LEDGER · Seven dimensions

**Flows:** Historical October power/heat damage and interrupted utility delivery in Ukraine; future contractual US LPG flows to India in 2027, not today's physical shipments.

**Dependencies:** Electricity supplies enable some heat and water services; India's domestic LPG system depends on imported cargoes, shipping terms and receiving capacity.

**Bottlenecks:** Damaged grids and heat assets, emergency power distribution and water pumping; India faces geographically concentrated LPG imports and maritime exposure.

**Substitutability:** Alternative generation, restoration and fuel sourcing require time and infrastructure; the reports do not provide universal durations or equivalent substitute volumes.

**Leverage:** Control over restoration resources and import diversification options matters; no quantified redistribution of bargaining power is established.

**Rent:** Alternative LPG freight and contract terms may redistribute costs; no measurable 17 November rent capture is available.

**Adaptation:** Ukraine implemented rolling outages following October attacks; India awarded prospective 2027 US LPG supply contracts to diversify sourcing. Neither changes should be counted as today's physical throughput.

## WHAT CHANGED — AND WHAT DID NOT

**Confirmed new physical system Δ dated 17 November: 0 at the sourcing cutoff.** October damage and casualties are historical observations released later. India made a new reported procurement commitment, but no LPG shipment is confirmed today. UN verification of October civilian casualties does not establish the cause of each individual death.
