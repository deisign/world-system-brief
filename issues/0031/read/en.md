# Commodity supply shocks compound across markets

In its October 28 Commodity Markets Outlook, the World Bank forecast a 23% increase in global commodity prices in 2026 and projected a 5.2% decline in global oil supply. These are model-based annual forecasts, not verified physical losses incurred on October 28.

## SYSTEM LEDGER

**Flow:** Energy, food and industrial commodities.

**Bottleneck:** Concurrent Middle East, Black Sea and weather disruptions.

**Substitution horizon:** Not established in the cited report.

**Leverage:** Contingent on route access, supplier options and verified risk information.

**Rent distribution:** Not independently measured.

**Adaptation:** Stocks, fiscal support and market adjustments remain costly.
