# The strait is still a bargaining chip

On October 18 Iran reiterated its earlier proposal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz within seven days if Washington accepted conditions including relief from maritime restrictions. That is a bargaining position, not operational restoration of the route. Separate reporting about China's proposed solar-manufacturing export restrictions is follow-up to a draft presented on Friday: publication on Sunday does not establish a new final control.
