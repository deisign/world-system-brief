# Saudi oil exports adapt across coasts

Saudi Arabia's August crude exports rose 7.9% month on month to 4.451 million barrels per day, data reported on October 21 showed. Use of east and west coastal terminals is a partial adaptation. The statistical period is August, not a newly commissioned October export route.
