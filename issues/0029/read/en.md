# A pipeline cut tests contractual buffers

Indonesia's energy minister said on October 26 that exports of pipeline gas to Singapore had been cut by 31 million standard cubic feet daily, around 8% of pipeline supply and 2% of Singapore's overall gas demand, while contractual minimums continued to be met.
