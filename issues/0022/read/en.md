# Exports carry China's uneven growth

China released third-quarter growth of 4.5% year on year on Monday, October 19, above a 4.4% Reuters consensus and up from 4.3% in the preceding quarter. Industrial output rose 5.5% year on year in September, while retail sales increased only 1.1%; property investment remains weak. This is a new measurement of an ongoing structural divergence: exports and manufacturing bear much of the growth burden while domestic consumption remains soft. The figures do not in themselves establish a new trade restriction.

## SYSTEM LEDGER

**Flow:** Manufactured goods, capital and export revenue.

**Bottleneck:** Weak retail demand and property sector.

**Substitution horizon:** Domestic fiscal support limited by demand transmission.

**Leverage:** External buyers and Chinese exporters.

**Rent distribution:** Export industries benefit more than households.

**Adaptation:** External demand carries domestic imbalances.
